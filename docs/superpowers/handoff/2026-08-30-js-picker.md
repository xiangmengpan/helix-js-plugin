## 关键架构事实(复述,新会话必读)

- **picker 管道**:JS 源表(`state::with_picker_sources`)→ `run` 调 items(同步数组/Promise `.then` 续体)→ `UiRequest::OpenPicker{source, rows}` → term `apply_ui_requests` 建 `Picker<PickerRow,()>`(overlaid 层)→ Enter 经 `invoke_action`/选中经 `invoke_preview` 桥回 JS(经 source 名查源表,payload 数组传参)
- **行格式**:数组行 cells=payload;对象行 `{cells, payload}` 分离(预览/动作数据)
- **preview 桥路径泄漏**:`PREVIEW_PATH` thread_local 槽缓存最近路径 + `Box::leak`(有界泄漏,同路径复用)
- **read_tree 实现**:`tree_matches` 纯函数(递归/目录先行/深度)+ spawn_async_op FsTree promise 管道(照 glob_async)
- **异步错误反馈**:items reject/行解析失败 → `push_message` → term 侧 set_status(任务 2 审查 I1 修复,不静默)
